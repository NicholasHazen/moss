# Ecosystem atlas identity

Fieldnotes 0.3 uses a geometric habitat mark, a meadow cover and a restrained
teal, sage and gold palette. Reading pages keep a quiet light background and
selectable text. Original labelled diagrams still explain the simulated rules;
the generated cover is decorative, not a map of an executed scenario.

The two PNG assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on
September 24, 2026 from an original course concept board. No third-party art,
stock illustration, external font or network asset is required at runtime.
The mark has transparency; the wordmark is live HTML text.

- [Habitat mark](assets/identity/atlas-mark-v1.png)
- [Meadow cover](assets/identity/atlas-meadow-v1.png)

The production prompts are recorded below so a later revision can preserve
the same visual intent. The concept board served only as a style reference.

## logo

Use case: background-extraction. Extract and refine ONLY the geometric habitat tile logo from this selected Moss Fieldnotes concept board. One isolated centered mark on a genuinely transparent background, no wordmark, no label, no swatches, no meadow illustration. Preserve the selected identity: three interlocking irregular rounded habitat tiles with a seedling and a winding white river in negative space. Simplify delicate interior grass lines to one bold blade so the mark is clear at 32 pixels. Flat opaque fills in deep teal #164F50, sage #91AF86, pale golden #F4D987. Perfectly sharp edges, solid colors, no texture, no bevel, no shadow, no gradients. Square canvas, generous clear padding, logo fills approximately 80% of the canvas. It should read as a modern ecosystem atlas logo, friendly but professional.

## hero

Use case: illustration-story. Create the standalone wide editorial meadow illustration for the selected Moss Fieldnotes ecosystem-atlas identity, using the attached board ONLY as a style/palette reference. Deliver ONLY the illustration; no logo, wordmark, palette swatches, captions, labels, borders or text. Wide panoramic landscape for the top of a website. A sophisticated flat illustrated atlas seen from directly overhead: interlocking meadow habitat patches, a winding pale blue stream, clusters of rounded dark teal trees, sage grassland, small pale yellow and apricot flowering patches, delicate contour lines and subtle fine grid. Two very small clean stylized animals, one hare and one fox, occupying different meadow patches. Make their silhouettes readable and believable. Crisp cut-paper-like silhouettes, soft mineral colors, subtle paper grain within shapes, no shadows or dimensional extrusion. Calm adult science-publication art with breathing room, meticulous composition, original shapes. Avoid childish clipart, realistic fur, game HUDs, icons, labels, gradients, glow, vignette, brand-sheet background. An inviting world to investigate. The illustration fills the canvas edge to edge; light ivory negative space integrated among habitats. Aspect ratio approximately 2.5:1.
